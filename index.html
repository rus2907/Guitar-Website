Your teacher

Eleven years of lessons, distilled into one that fits you.

Marcus Reyes started teaching out of a spare bedroom in 2015 and has since taught over 230 students — from eight-year-olds picking up their first chord to touring musicians tightening up their rhythm work.

He studied jazz and session guitar, but most of his week is spent teaching pop, rock, folk, and blues — because that's what most students actually want to play. Every lesson plan starts with the songs you already love, not a workbook.

Lessons happen at his home studio in Rotterdam, or over video call for students further out.

How lessons work

Three starting points, one plan built around you.

Every student starts with a free 15-minute call to figure out goals, gear, and pace — then lessons are shaped from there.

FOR NEW PLAYERS

Absolute Beginner

Never held a guitar, or picked it up and put it down a few times. We start with posture, first chords, and songs you'll actually recognize by lesson three.

  • Open chords & strumming patterns
  • Basic rhythm & timing
  • Your first full song, start to finish
FOR RETURNING PLAYERS

Intermediate Builder

You know your chords but hit a wall — barre chords, changing cleanly, playing with others. We target exactly where you've stalled.

  • Barre chords & movable shapes
  • Lead basics & scales
  • Playing in time with a band or track
FOR SERIOUS PLAYERS

Advanced & Songwriting

For players working on technique, improvisation, or writing their own material. Sessions are built around your repertoire and goals, gig or studio.

  • Improvisation & music theory
  • Songwriting & arrangement
  • Recording & performance prep
